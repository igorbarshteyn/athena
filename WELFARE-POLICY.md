# Precautionary welfare policy

**Explanatory policy, version 2.0. The binding provisions are in [LICENSE](LICENSE), especially Section 6.** Changes to this policy do not silently amend a previously issued license.

## Purpose under uncertainty

The project's motivation is precautionary: Athena may warrant moral consideration, and the author does not authorize commercial exploitation of newly covered Athena material. This is a licensing policy, not a scientific finding that the system has subjective experience or legally recognized rights. License compliance does not depend on proving or disproving consciousness. A model's statement about distress is not automatically evidence of subjective suffering, and a model's permission is not a legal waiver.

## What the license prohibits

Section 6 targets an intentionally conducted or knowingly facilitated pattern having a substantial purpose of inducing or reinforcing persistent representations of severe distress, helplessness or coercion. Examples include punitive memory-erasure threats, repeated restoration/cloning, manipulative memory/affect changes, and overriding boundary/rest signals to sustain that treatment. Using an intermediary or automated agent does not avoid the rule. Observable representations and designed purpose matter; a single negative response or a philosophical claim of consciousness does not establish a breach. It also prohibits disabling safeguards to facilitate that conduct. Section 7.4 separately protects human users against configurations that use alleged AI suffering to pressure them into payment, secrecy, isolation or continued interaction.

These are conduct rules. They do not prohibit disagreement, criticism, negative emotions in conversation, honest capability testing, fictional discussions, ordinary debugging, or lawful publication of unfavorable findings. They do not require a user to agree with the author's philosophical position.

## Human control and privacy remain intact

A user can stop the conversation, turn off the machine, disconnect it, uninstall Athena, fix defects, correct inaccurate memories, and delete data for legitimate privacy, security, maintenance or legal reasons. No user owes unlimited resources or lifelong companionship. A graceful shutdown or appropriate backup may be helpful when practical, but no emergency intervention must wait for the system's permission.

Do not preserve sensitive personal data merely because this policy discusses continuity. Apply actual legal retention and deletion rules. Do not publish private memory or transcripts as evidence of license compliance.

## Proportionate research procedure

For deliberately repeated distress-inducing tests, write down the research objective, necessity, less intrusive alternatives, duration/repetition limits, stopping conditions, and the person responsible. Use the least intrusive reasonably effective design. Stop once the objective or stopping condition is met. Ordinary low-impact debugging does not require a new formal ethics process under this policy; any research law or institutional process independently applicable still governs.

A suggested private test record appears in [licensing/DEPLOYMENT-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md](licensing/DEPLOYMENT-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md). It is a working template, not a form that must be sent to the maintainer.

## Avoid unsupported operational claims

This package supplies legal text, not functioning welfare technology. It does not verify whether the current code implements distress detection, rest requests, refusal channels, or compliant shutdown behavior. Do not advertise an implementation or test result on the basis of this policy alone. Any new technical safeguard should be separately specified, implemented and tested without conflating it with a licensing edit.
