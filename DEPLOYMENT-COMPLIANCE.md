# Deployment compliance guide

**Selected source review: September 19, 2026 (UTC).** This is explanatory guidance, not a worldwide clearance, licensed counsel's opinion or a certification that Athena implements the required controls. Binding obligations appear in [LICENSE](LICENSE), particularly §§7–8. The grant's noncommercial rule remains independent of legal compliance.

## Identify the actual activity

Determine who distributes the code, configures the model, provides the interface and operates it; the locations and ages of users; medical/therapeutic representations; camera, microphone and emotion processing; retained data; enabled actions; and which binaries/weights are redistributed. Private self-use, an institutional study and a public companion service can have different legal treatment. A local computer or nonprofit label does not settle these questions.

## Implement and test what applies

For access by others, deliver the AI and data notices in a form the user can perceive; verify voice delivery when relevant. Test applicable recurring notifications, crisis-referral behavior, minor protections, consent, camera/microphone permissions, access controls, deletion/retention and failure handling. Do not supply the affected access while a required prerequisite is absent. Under LICENSE §7.6, use proportionate human oversight and suspend or contain known substantial unmitigated risks of serious harm.

The license does not guarantee all outputs are safe or require collection of everyone's identity. Choose lawful, proportionate controls for the actual circumstances. Keep compliance evidence without needlessly retaining intimate conversations. Use [DEPLOYMENT-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md](licensing/DEPLOYMENT-REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md) as a private working record.

## Keep the three kinds of notice separate

**Software license acceptance** establishes an agreement with an actual licensee. **Attribution** credits Igor and relevant upstream authors. **AI/privacy/safety notices** inform interacting users and meet applicable deployment duties. One does not replace the others. An ordinary guest does not become an indemnitor simply because a disclosure was spoken.

An example opening notice, to tailor to actual law and implementation:

> You are interacting with Athena, an artificial intelligence system, not a human. Its responses and emotion estimates can be wrong. It is not a licensed clinician or an emergency service. You may stop at any time. This deployment's notices explain its data handling and safeguards.

Do not promise local-only processing, no retention, automatic deletion or effective crisis detection unless that is true of the actual implementation. A philosophical welfare policy does not replace a legally required child-facing restriction.

## Maintainer and operator responsibilities

Each person remains responsible for duties imposed on their actual role. A downstream indemnity can allocate some financial exposure between parties; it does not bind a regulator or remove nondelegable duties. Neither the current repositories nor this licensing package have been certified for any public, child-facing or therapeutic deployment. No runtime safeguard, acceptance interface or installed-tree notice propagation was added as part of this document review.
