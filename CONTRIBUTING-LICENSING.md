## New contributions

New contributions should expressly identify the permissions being supplied. A suggested pull-request statement is:

> I intentionally submit the identified contribution for inclusion in this project under the Athena Noncommercial, Attribution and Responsible Deployment License, version 2.0, only to the extent I control the relevant rights. I have identified included third-party material, applicable licenses, and any employer, client or other permissions required for this submission. I am not assigning copyright or granting a general right to relicense the contribution commercially.

Do not make that statement for material whose ownership or permissions You have not established. A contribution without an express, adequate grant is not automatically cleared because it was submitted through GitHub. The maintainer should resolve its status before merging it. No unmodified upstream file should be relabeled as solely Athena-owned.

## Information to provide

Identify the paths changed, the origin of incorporated code, the upstream project and commit when known, the license of copied or adapted material, and notices that must be retained. Flag third-party libraries, images, voices, model weights, datasets and generated files rather than treating them all as original source. Identify employer or client rights where relevant. Disclose material AI assistance and human editing/provenance sufficient to assess rights; disclosure does not itself determine copyrightability.

## Existing contributors and historical releases

This procedure is prospective. It is not a retroactive contributor agreement, proof of ownership, or an assignment from earlier authors. Existing grants and contributor permissions must be reviewed separately. Maintainers cannot use this file to override another author's conditions.

## Security and privacy

Do not submit secrets, private transcripts, personal memories, identifiable voice samples without permission, or unnecessary personal data. Ask for an appropriate private reporting route before sharing a sensitive issue; this package does not invent or promise an existing confidential intake service.

## Metadata and header changes

For new, independently authored files intentionally licensed under this version, a custom marker can be used:

```text
SPDX-License-Identifier: LicenseRef-Athena-NCARD-2.0
```

A custom `LicenseRef` is not an official SPDX-listed license. For mixed-origin files, first establish the applicable component-level rights and retain upstream notices. Do not mechanically replace MIT, Apache, BSD or other headers throughout the tree. Do not write `MIT OR LicenseRef-Athena-NCARD-2.0` for newly restricted material: that would offer an MIT alternative. A build's component inventory is usually clearer than a misleading single expression applied to every file.
